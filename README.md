**Problem Statement**

AtliQ Grands is a well-known hotel chain in India that has been experiencing increasing competition and a decline in revenue. To understand the reasons behind these challenges, the management wants to make better use of data and business insights. As the company does not have an internal data analytics team, external analytical support has been taken to study the data, identify important trends, and suggest strategies for improving revenue and customer retention.

**Project Goal**

The main objective of this project is to analyze hotel-related data using Python and identify meaningful business insights. The analysis aims to help AtliQ Grands understand its performance, identify areas for improvement, and make more informed business decisions.

**Project Highlights**

Data Cleaning – Identified and handled missing values and outliers.

Data Transformation – Created additional columns, including occupancy percentage.

Data Analysis and Insights – Answered important business questions such as:

Calculated the average occupancy rate by room type and city.

Compared occupancy rates between weekdays and weekends.

Analyzed revenue generated across different cities.

Studied monthly revenue trends.

Examined revenue generated through different booking platforms.

**Skills Learned**
Python Programming: Improved my understanding of Python for data cleaning, transformation, analysis, and visualization.

Data Visualization: Used the Matplotlib library to create different charts and visualizations.

Pandas: Worked with DataFrames, grouping and aggregation, concatenation, merging datasets, handling missing values, and reading CSV files.
